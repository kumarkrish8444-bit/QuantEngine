SETUP
How do I get the Search Engine ID?
  1. Open Google Programmable Search Engine.
  2. Create a search engine and configure it to search the web, if that option is available for your account.
  3. Open its setup / overview page and copy the Search Engine ID (usually called cx).
  4. Paste the ID above and press Connect. The ID is saved in this browser only.

A Search Engine ID is required for embedded Google results. Results and coverage depend on your Google search-engine configuration. This demo does not claim to have Google's private ranking systems or a general-purpose AI model connected.
